## System Requirements

| Specification | Minimum | Recommended |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Processor** | Quad-core @ 2.0 GHz+ | 6th Gen Intel® Core / AMD Ryzen™ 1000-series or later |
| **Memory** | 8 GiB | 16 GiB |
| **Storage** | 30 GB | 60 GB |

> **Note:** Nix primarily supports **Ubuntu 22.04+** for LibreLane workflows.
